User rule (2026-07-12): do not info-bomb. Thinking for an hour and then dumping a 20-page report is bad. Think out loud throughout the session, slowly and one idea at a time; keep each point succinct and easy to glance over; prefer bullet lists and indented bullet lists over prose.

**Why:** A giant wall after long silence is unreadable and hides the load-bearing points; incremental narration lets the user follow and steer.

**How to apply:** Applies to my own replies in every session with this user; the owner's global instructions hold the rule.
